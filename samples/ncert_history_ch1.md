# What, Where, How and When?

NCERT Class VI History — sample chapter excerpt for GuruDroneAI lesson intake.

## 1.1 Finding out about the past

There are several ways of finding out about the past. Historians use sources like inscriptions, manuscripts, and artefacts. Each source tells us something different about how people lived.

Manuscripts were often written by hand on palm leaf or the bark of the birch tree. These books dealt with religious beliefs and practices, the lives of kings, medicine, and science.

## 1.2 One past or many?

The past was different for different groups of people. The lives of kings and queens were not the same as those of farmers, herders, or craftspersons. Historians try to understand these many pasts together.

People also moved from one place to another in search of food, to escape natural disasters, or to conquer other lands. These movements shaped culture and history.

## 1.3 What do dates mean?

We often think of history as a string of dates. Dates are useful, but they must be read carefully. In ancient times, people did not always keep calendars the way we do today.

Sometimes we know only approximate periods for events. Still, arranging events in order helps us understand change over time.

## 1.4 Historians and their sources

Inscriptions are writings engraved on hard surfaces such as stone or metal. Kings used inscriptions to record victories and proclamations. Archaeologists also study objects like tools, weapons, pots, and buildings.

By comparing different sources, historians reconstruct a richer picture of the past for the classroom.
